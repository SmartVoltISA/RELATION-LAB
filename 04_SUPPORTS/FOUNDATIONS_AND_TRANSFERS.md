### Ω-REL-001 — Independent Relation Components

Original status in Ω-Lab: HYPOTHESIS / RESEARCH PROTOCOL — NOT EXECUTED.

Research question: whether the minimal relation can be represented by two directional observable responses `(r_AB, r_BA)`, or whether an additional independently variable component is required.

Important rule transferred unchanged: a zero observable response must not automatically mean absence of a relation. Existence and response remain distinct variables.

Source: Ω-Lab `research/RELATIONS/OMEGA-REL-001-INDEPENDENT-RELATION-COMPONENTS.md`.

### Relation Duality

The Ω-Lab relation-duality hypothesis proposes potentially independent directional contributions `w(A→B)` and `w(B→A)` and competing models from a symmetric scalar to a history-dependent relation.

This remains a hypothesis until a simpler competing model is unable to reproduce an observed result.

Source: Ω-Lab `research/RELATIONS/RELATION-DUALITY-2026-08-13.md`.

## Transfer rule
