# Relation hypotheses v0.1

## H1 — Relation is operationally recoverable

A relation between distinguishable states can be inferred from reproducible changes in transition behavior.

Status: SUPPORTED IN SYNTHETIC MODELS; NOT PHYSICAL PROOF.

## H2 — Relation may be directional

`K_AB` and `K_BA` may vary independently.

Status: OPEN.

## H3 — Relation may be dynamic

The relation can be represented as `K_ij(t)` rather than a constant edge.

Status: SUPPORTED IN SYNTHETIC TIME-VARYING TEST.

## H4 — Relation may change sign

A relation can change from positive influence to negative influence without the elements disappearing.

Status: SUPPORTED IN SYNTHETIC TIME-VARYING TEST.

## H5 — Observable dependence is not sufficient to establish direct relation

Correlation can arise from indirect paths or common causes.

Status: SUPPORTED BY CONTROL MODELS.

## H6 — A relation need not be geometric

Dynamical influence can be recovered in systems where geometric adjacency is not the defining criterion.

Status: OPEN / REQUIRES FURTHER CONTROLS.

## H7 — Minimal relation may require more than two directional responses

The existing Ω-REL-001 hypothesis asks whether `(r_AB, r_BA)` is sufficient or whether an independent relational component is required.

Status: OPEN; original protocol was explicitly not executed in Ω-Lab.
